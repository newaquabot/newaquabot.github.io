AquaBot

A robot that learns when each plant needs water, so no plant gets too much or too little.

Watering plant by plant

On a large farm it is hard to check every plant by eye, and small changes in the soil go unnoticed. AquaBot keeps track of the soil around each plant so watering can match what each plant actually needs.

Less guesswork means less wasted water and healthier crops.

How AquaBot learns

AquaBot does more than sense dry soil. It learns each plant's pattern over time.

  1. It measuresAquaBot keeps recording the soil moisture around each plant.
  2. It noticesIt learns how quickly the soil around each plant dries, and spots unusual changes in that drying rate.
  3. It predictsIt works out when each individual plant will need watering.
  4. It remembersIt keeps track of which watering methods have been most effective.

What is inside

Each part was designed to work together to perform a specific task.

  • MotorsPower the robot's movement.
  • WheelsCarry AquaBot across the field.
  • SensorsRead the soil moisture around each plant.
  • Control systemJoins the parts together and runs our code.
