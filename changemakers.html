Changemakers Award

Technology that helps farmers use less water and grow more.

Farming should not be left behind

Technology is moving fast in cities, and agriculture must keep up. With more urbanisation, a growing population and fewer people to work the land, farmers need smarter and more efficient ways to manage their fields.

Large farms are difficult to monitor by hand, and even small changes in the soil can go unnoticed. That can mean wasted crops, lower yields and unnecessary water use.

We believe technology should empower farmers by reducing effort, cutting errors and making agriculture more sustainable.

Our answer: AquaBot

AquaBot learns how its field behaves, so watering can be planned for each plant.

  1. It recordsAquaBot continuously records soil moisture levels.
  2. It learnsIt learns how quickly the soil around each plant dries.
  3. It spots problemsIt uses this history to identify unusual changes in drying rates.
  4. It predicts and remembersIt predicts when each plant will need water and remembers which watering methods worked best.

Goals we support

AquaBot connects to three of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

SDG 6

Clean water and sanitation

Reduces unnecessary water consumption through precise, plant-specific irrigation.

SDG 2

Zero hunger

Promotes healthier crops, minimises potential crop losses and improves agricultural productivity.

SDG 12

Responsible consumption and production

Encourages efficient use of water and agricultural resources.
