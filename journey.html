The journey of building our robot

How we turned our idea into a working robot.

From idea to working robot

Making the robot was a long task. From designing to assembling the parts, building the robot and finally programming it, we learnt many new things.

We planned the robot and designed each part to work together and perform a specific task. We then carefully put together the motors, sensors, wheels and control system, and wrote the code that lets them work as one robot.

Through planning, building and testing, we turned our idea into a working robot. It was created by combining creativity, innovation and technology.

It could not have been done without every team member.
